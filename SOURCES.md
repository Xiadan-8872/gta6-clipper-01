# Sources

All footage in `clip.mp4` is raw footage cut by the agent in this session
from Rockstar Games' official GTA 6 Trailer 2, downloaded with yt-dlp from
Rockstar's official YouTube channel. No finished clip made by anyone else
was used; the cut, the vertical reframe and the on-screen text were all
done here with ffmpeg.
